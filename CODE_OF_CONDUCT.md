# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Argue about the
work, not the person.

## What is expected

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, and any other
space it uses, is expected to:

- Give and take criticism about the work without making it personal
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution and say why once
- Respect that people have different levels of experience with macOS, Swift, and
  accessibility work
- Keep discussion on the technical question at hand

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or sustained disruption
- Comments about a person's identity rather than their argument
- Publishing someone's private information
- Unwelcome attention of a sexual nature

## Reporting

Report a problem to the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are read
by the maintainer and are not shared further without the reporter's agreement.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, and may block an account, when
this document is not followed. Where practical the reason is stated.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project uses, and to anyone representing the
project elsewhere.
